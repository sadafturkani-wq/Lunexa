# ✨ Lunexa — Personal Lifestyle Dashboard

**Organize your day. Track your goals. Become your best self.**

Lunexa is a personal lifestyle dashboard designed to help users organize their daily routines, track personal goals, and find inspiration to become their best selves. With a modern and elegant interface, Lunexa brings everyday organization and personal growth into one convenient space.

## 🌟 Features

- 📅 **Daily Organization** — Organize your day and manage everyday activities.
- 🎯 **Goal Tracking** — Keep track of personal goals and monitor your progress.
- ✨ **Daily Inspiration** — Discover motivational messages to start your day positively.
- 🌱 **Personal Growth** — Build productive routines and encourage better habits.
- 🎨 **Modern Design** — Enjoy a clean, elegant, and user-friendly interface.
- 📱 **Responsive Layout** — Designed to work across different screen sizes.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
